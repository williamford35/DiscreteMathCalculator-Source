Welcome to Discrete Mathematics Calculator! Discrete Mathematics Calculator is intended for use in discrete mathematics, data structures,and algorithm study. For discrete mathematics, it implements number theoretic calculations such as modular arithmetic and many counting functions such as permutations, combinations, the Catalan numbers, and the Bell numbers. It also generates important and interesting triangles, such as Pascal's triangle, and the Bell triangle. The calculator also computes and enumerates subsets and set partitions. As an additional feature, the calculator factors large integers, demonstrates RSA encryption, Diffie-Hellman key exchange, digital signatures, and the discrete logarithm problem.

For data structures and algorithms, it draws and performs operations for a number of binary trees, including balanced trees, Huffman trees, and expression trees. General trees can be drawn and traversed, and the equivalent binary tree drawn. Editable graphs, both undirected and directed, with or without parallel edges can be drawn with optional weighted edges. Once a graph is in its final state, a number of graph algorithms are available, including DFS/BFS, topological sort, the computation of strong components, determination of minimal paths, finding maximum flow in a network, locating a maximal independent edge set, and searching for Euler cycles and paths. For small graphs, NP complete problems such as TSP, finding a clique, locating Hamiltonian cycles and paths, and determining a vertex cover and independent set are also available.

The software can define and graph relations and also draw the transitive, symmetric, and reflexive closure of a relation. The user can graph the Hasse diagram for the powerset of a set of up to size six and the Hasse diagram of the divisibility relation. As an example of a total order permutations can be listed in lexicographic order.

The Galois field GF(2^m) has many interesting properties, including its use in AES encryption.The software performs numerous operations on the polynomials with any modulus.

Elliptic curves have applications in encryption and are commonly used for key exchange and digital signatures. The software executes numerous operations on elliptic curves in the field GF(p), including curve plotting for small p that includes a point plot of the integer values in GF(p), scalar multiplication, addition, generation of a random verifiable curve, creation of secure curve domain, and the discrete logarithm.
